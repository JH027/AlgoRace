Welcome to Algorace, a platform that encourages you to practice your programming skills through some friendly competition!





1. Add Friends

Find friends to track programming progress together.

2. Choose a Challenge

Choose a coding problem solve and race friends.

3. Race and Compare

Race for speed, then compare your solutions for more details like time and space complexity. Points are calculated based on time it took to solve the problem, and time and space complexity.
